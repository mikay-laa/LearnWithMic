Active Recall

Testing yourself without looking at notes strengthens memory retention far more effectively than passive re-reading.

Spaced Repetition

Click to flip ⚡

Reviewing study material at increasing intervals (e.g., 1 day, 3 days, 1 week) moves concepts into long-term memory.
